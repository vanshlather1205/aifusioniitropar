🌌 Project NEXUS

PROJECT NEXUS is an all-in-one smart campus companion designed to simplify student life by bringing academics, communication, navigation, and campus services into a single unified platform.

Prepared by Vansh and Vibhor

🚀 Overview

ProjectNova aims to enhance the everyday campus experience by integrating real-time information, intelligent email handling, academic tools, navigation services, and peer-to-peer collaboration features. From checking today’s mess menu to booking a cab, managing assignments, or finding lost belongings, ProjectNova acts as a digital nerve center for students.
